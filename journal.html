Abstract

Standing conclusion · version 2026-08-24

SELF/FUNDING is not yet an observed self-financing agent. It is a bounded research program asking whether one machine-verifiable software service can receive real payment through an accountable operator, fulfill or refund the obligation, capture every real cost, and finance some of its own future computation.

Current infrastructure supports tool-using agents, model routing, programmatic payments, sandboxing, idempotency, and reconciliation. It does not yet prove durable customer demand, positive repeated contribution, independent agent identity, or a surplus large enough to subsidize free human access.[1][2][3]

2026-08-23Round 01 · foundation audit
Evidence audit

The thesis separates into five claims

Bounded agent work and routing have meaningful engineering support. Positive task margin requires project-specific measurement. Persistent machine demand and a treasury large enough to subsidize free frontier intelligence remain unproven.[1][2][3][10]

  • Selected first service hypothesis: improve an existing test suite under deterministic verification.
  • Rejected shortcut: ten successful tasks cannot establish a low failure rate.
  • Correction: a Postgres journal may be append-only and tamper-evident, but is not inherently immutable.

Next: determine the smallest legally accountable money lifecycle for the first paid job.

2026-08-23Round 02 · priority reset
Product direction

Follow the money after payment

A displayed balance is not proof of settled funds. A ledger entry is not the external rail. Revenue is not available surplus until delivery, provider costs, refund exposure, taxes, and required reserves are accounted for.

  • Research priority moved from abstract revenue growth to delivery, custody, authority, spending, reconciliation, and recovery.
  • The website replaced unlabeled margin scenarios with explicit targets and illustrative states.

Next: identify the operator, custody account, signer, refund path, and emergency controls.

Direct fact

Stripe delegates operations; it does not grant personhood

Stripe still requires an identifiable User and merchant of record. A verified operator can let software create bounded payment objects and read events, but the agent does not own the account or funds. Connect moves merchant responsibility between platform and connected account; it does not remove it.[4][5][6][7]

  • ACP and Shared Payment Tokens primarily support buyer-agent authorization while the business remains seller and merchant.
  • U.S. federal law recognizes electronic-agent involvement when its action is legally attributable to the person being bound.[8]
  • Current path: operator owns obligations; agent acts under narrow, auditable permissions.

2026-08-24Round 04 · program design
Two horizons

Execute now; specify the missing future

The project now runs two linked queues. Horizon A must produce a buildable decision, experiment, result, or exact blocker. Horizon B names what fails today, the missing primitive, the evidence threshold that would prove it exists, and a bridge experiment.

  • Current reference path: verified operator, Stripe, narrow service, bounded permissions, real refund, full-cost accounting, reconciliation.
  • Frontier requirements: machine-attributable identity, policy-native custody, portable mandates, liability, insurance, dispute, insolvency, attestation, governance, and recovery.
  • Machine-readable payment protocols are relevant rail primitives, but infrastructure availability is not evidence of demand or economic closure.[9]
  • Cadence: advance at least two current-path questions for each frontier scan.

Next: complete the Stripe money state machine, then implement the smallest real control-plane prototype.

00:42:22 UTCRound 05 · money state machine
Direct fact + bounded inference

A successful card payment is not one final state

PaymentIntent success permits fulfillment, but the linked net funds can still be pending. Stripe availability permits debit and payout, but refund, dispute, and payout-failure exposure remains. The buildable conclusion is to track payment flow, external custody, customer obligation, and internal allocation as separate dimensions.[11][12][13]

  • Counterevidence: Stripe explicitly supports fulfillment at PaymentIntent success; waiting for bank payout is not a universal requirement. If customer funds are still pending, however, paid provider work needs separately confirmed working capital.
  • State regression: a refund may later fail, and a payout shown as paid can change to failed within up to five additional business days.[14][16]
  • Unknown: account-specific reserves, legal segregation, revenue recognition, and reserve sizing still require live evidence and US legal/accounting review.
  • Experiment: replay Stripe's dispute, asynchronous-refund, available-balance, duplicate-event, and out-of-order-event fixtures before one small live payment and real refund.[17][18][19]
