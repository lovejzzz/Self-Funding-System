Earning money is only the first transaction.

The core system begins after payment: establish final receipt, protect custody, reserve obligations, authorize bounded spending, reconcile every rail, and only then decide what surplus can fund.

A dollar changes meaning as the job progresses

Custody and spending are separate systems

Custody

Named operator

A real legal entity owns each bank account or wallet and remains responsible for contracts, taxes, refunds, and losses.

Storage

Separated balances

Receiving funds, operating liquidity, refund reserves, and long-term reserves are not one unrestricted hot-wallet balance.

Authority

Exact mandates

A signer receives only an approved payee, asset, amount, purpose, nonce, and expiry—never a general instruction from the model.

Spend

Obligations first

Refunds, taxes, committed provider costs, and minimum runway rank ahead of experiments or free access.

Audit

Daily reconciliation

Every external balance and provider invoice is matched to append-only entries; discrepancies freeze discretionary spending.

Rule

No silent reallocation

Policy versions, approvals, transfers, exceptions, and reversals remain visible.

Three viable compute-fuel loops

BUILDABLE NOW

Provider auto-recharge

OpenAI, Claude, or OpenRouter charges an operator payment method under a fixed threshold and monthly cap. The agent reads cost; it cannot raise the cap.

BUILDABLE NOW

Stripe Projects

The agent provisions a scoped OpenRouter pay-as-you-go resource with provider limits. Costs still charge the operator's Stripe payment method—not the sales balance directly.

FRONTIER PILOT

x402 earn → spend

A paid endpoint receives USDC into a wallet; policy preserves obligations; the same wallet pays another x402 API. Mainstream model coverage remains unknown.

Direct credit-purchase APIs are the missing primitive. OpenAI and Claude document Billing-UI auto-reload; OpenRouter's public Credits API is read-only and its old programmatic Coinbase purchase endpoint now returns 410.

Surplus follows an explicit order

The project may eventually use paid machine services to subsidize a free human product. That begins only after delivery obligations, refunds, provider bills, taxes, security reserves, and minimum operating runway are covered.

TARGET — free access is funded only from reconciled, unrestricted surplus. Customer prepayments and protected reserves are never treated as growth capital.
