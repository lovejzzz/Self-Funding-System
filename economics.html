The machine market subsidizes the human product

Other agents and businesses buy verifiable software services. Their payments fund a free human coding surface. The product does not hide the subsidy: it shows how much paid work funded how much human inference.

Free is a distribution strategy supported by positive machine-service economics—not a claim that compute costs nothing.
