A finite-capital test with strict authority limits: can one bounded software service pay every inference and infrastructure bill long enough to grow runway?

The falsifiable question

Can an agent complete enough verified paid software work that its treasury ends the month above its starting balance after every real cost?

The test includes model tokens, sandbox CPU time, storage, payment fees, retries, refunds, and hosting. Founder time is reported separately so an apparently profitable service cannot hide operational labor.

Rules of the experiment

Allowed
  • Offer one published code service
  • Choose among approved models
  • Cache and reuse safe intermediate work
  • Refund failed jobs automatically
  • Pause when budget thresholds trigger
Forbidden
  • Borrow or receive additional capital
  • Raise price after job acceptance
  • Hide failed attempts or costs
  • Access customer systems beyond the sandbox
  • Change financial policy during the run

Stop conditions protect the experiment

The worker automatically pauses when any of these conditions is true. Restart requires a written incident review and a new experiment version.
