Prove money and non-cash capacity on separate evidence rails.

The falsifiable question

Can one operator-controlled agent service receive a real payment, protect every obligation, deliver or refund, capture every cost, and reconcile to zero unexplained difference?

This comes before a 30-day solvency claim. The test includes model tokens, sandbox usage, storage, payment fees, retries, refunds, hosting, donated credits, and operator time.

The parallel resource-rights question

Can one operator-owned compute allowance be issued, scoped, redeemed, measured, exhausted or revoked, and reconciled to the provider record without becoming cash or expanding agent authority?

Rules of the experiment

Allowed
  • Offer one published code service
  • Choose among approved models
  • Cache and reuse safe intermediate work
  • Refund failed jobs automatically
  • Pause when budget thresholds trigger
Forbidden
  • Borrow or receive additional capital
  • Raise price after job acceptance
  • Hide failed attempts or costs
  • Access customer systems beyond the sandbox
  • Change financial policy during the run

Stop conditions protect the experiment

The worker automatically pauses when any of these conditions is true. Restart requires source evidence, an append-only correction when needed, a clean rerun, and independent approval.
