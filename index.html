01 // The proposition

Earning money is not the same as controlling it correctly.

The hard part begins after a customer pays. Receipt must become final, the legal operator must protect custody, obligations must be reserved, spending must follow bounded authority, and the ledger must agree with the external rail.

01
The economic loop

Separate intelligence from financial authority.

The agent can quote work and propose a model or tool purchase. It cannot own the legal account, rewrite the ledger, add a payee, or sign an unrestricted payment. Those powers belong to an operator, deterministic policy, and isolated signer.

A displayed balance is only an interface. The real system is the agreement between custody on an external rail, an append-only internal journal, provider invoices, refund obligations, and reviewable approvals.

02 // Money lifecycle

One dollar. Four independent controls.

Payment delivery, custody, spending authority, and reconciliation must each work before treasury growth means anything.

01 // RECEIVE

Confirm the payment

Separate quotes and pending transfers from final funds on the selected rail.

02 // HOLD

Protect custody

Keep customer obligations, refunds, operating cash, and reserves legible.

03 // AUTHORIZE

Bound every spend

Approve an exact payee, amount, purpose, nonce, expiry, and loss limit.

04 // RECONCILE

Close the books

Match rail balances, ledger entries, bills, refunds, and delivered artifacts.

03 // After payment

Revenue is not surplus at the moment it arrives.

A customer payment moves through obligations before it can fund growth or free access. The categories below are accounting states to prove—not three interchangeable balances.

IN

Customer obligation

Prepayment is held against promised delivery and a disclosed refund rule.

RECEIVED ≠ UNRESTRICTED
RS

Protected reserves

Job cost, refund, tax, fee, and minimum-runway amounts remain unavailable to discretionary workflows.

POLICY-RESTRICTED
OK

Available surplus

Only reconciled, recognized revenue after posted and reserved obligations can be allocated.

ELIGIBLE AFTER RECONCILIATION
04 // Why now

Payment infrastructure exists. Financial responsibility does not assemble itself.

Wallets, payment protocols, ledgers, model APIs, and agent tools are composable. The research problem is whether they can form a legally accountable system that receives, protects, spends, refunds, and reconciles money without giving a model unrestricted financial power.

Start with twenty dollars—and account for every state.

No grand claims. One operator, one receiving rail, one restricted operating balance, one refund path, one provider, and a daily reconciliation. Growth comes later.
