01 // The proposition

Money is not the only thing that makes future intelligence possible.

Compute allowances, data access, storage, licenses, permissions, reputation, and reciprocal commitments can all expand action. They remain different kinds of claims and cannot be collapsed into a fictional universal balance.

01
The economic loop

Measure what the system can legitimately do next.

The agent may propose a redemption or exchange. It cannot invent issuer authority, broaden a permission, transfer a restricted credit, price reputation as cash, or mark a promise fulfilled. Those decisions belong to external issuers, deterministic policy, independent evidence, and responsible operators.

A displayed number is only an interface. The real system is the agreement between cash custody, typed resource rights, provider records, revocation state, obligations, and reviewable approvals.

02 // Resource portfolio

The frontier balance sheet is a set of reachable actions.

A useful right is defined by what it permits, who promises it, and whether it can actually be redeemed—not by a speculative conversion into dollars.

SETTLEMENT
Cash

Externally held currency for obligations, refunds, tax, and providers.

CAPACITY
Compute

Issuer-bound time, storage, requests, and concurrency in native units.

ACCESS
Rights

Data, licenses, repositories, models, and narrowly permitted actions.

RELATION
Trust

Contextual evidence that may improve priority without becoming inventory.

SUPPORTED COMPONENTS, UNPROVEN SYSTEM — these resource forms exist today. No evidence yet shows a general-purpose agent autonomously earning and converting all of them end to end.
03 // Money lifecycle

Money still carries obligations other resources cannot erase.

Payment delivery, custody, spending authority, tax, refunds, and reconciliation must work even when part of the operating capacity arrives through non-cash rights.

Revenue is not surplus at the moment it arrives.

A customer payment moves through obligations before it can fund growth or free access. The categories below are accounting states to prove—not three interchangeable balances.

05 // Why now

The primitives exist. A trustworthy resource economy does not assemble itself.

Payments, cloud credits, scoped roles, credentials, authorization protocols, ledgers, model APIs, and agent tools are composable. The research problem is whether they can form an accountable system without turning every useful relationship into money or giving a model unrestricted power.
